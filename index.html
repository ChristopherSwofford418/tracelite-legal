Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026
Developer: ClearPass Technologies LLC
Contact: christopher.swofford@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Ritual Reset handles information when you use the iOS app. Ritual Reset is designed to analyze supported content on your device and minimize transmission of personal information.

Information Ritual Reset accesses

Ritual Reset may access photos and videos you authorize through Apple’s Photo Library permission to prepare a focused review of screenshots, large media, screen recordings, likely duplicates, visually similar photos, and blur candidates. Ritual Reset may access contacts only after you authorize Contacts access to identify possible duplicate and incomplete contacts and to perform merges you explicitly approve.

Photos, videos, and contacts are processed on your device. Ritual Reset does not upload those items, thumbnails, perceptual hashes, contact fields, or local identifiers to the developer’s servers.

On-device storage

Ritual Reset stores preferences, scan timestamps, derived analysis scores, local asset identifiers, similarity-group identifiers, and content-free decision summaries in its private app container. It does not create duplicate copies of your photos or videos.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

Product analytics and crash diagnostics are disabled unless you opt in in the app. If enabled, Ritual Reset may send product-interaction events and diagnostics through configured providers. Those events must not include media content, thumbnails, hashes, filenames, Photo Library identifiers, contact identifiers, names, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, or notes. You can withdraw consent in Ritual Reset Settings.

Purchases and notifications

Ritual Reset Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive payment-card details. If you enable weekly reminders, Ritual Reset schedules one local notification on your device; it does not continuously scan your library in the background.

Deletion and contact changes

Media deletion uses Apple’s Photo Library APIs and Apple’s confirmation flow. Contact merges use Apple’s Contacts framework after an in-app confirmation. Ritual Reset can generate a local vCard backup before a selected merge.

Accounts, advertising, and children

Ritual Reset does not require an account, does not sell personal information, does not use personal information for cross-app tracking or third-party advertising, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Ritual Reset is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children.

Your choices

You can limit or revoke Photo Library, Contacts, and notification access in iOS Settings. You can disable optional analytics in Ritual Reset Settings. Privacy questions may be sent to christopher.swofford@gmail.com.
